## Patterns that keep repeating

1. Operators reject black-box scores faster than incomplete but honest briefs.  
2. Integrations and reporting are where prescription becomes real.  
3. Silent failure in retrieval destroys trust more than slow answers.  
4. Override is often a feature in regulated work, not a model failure.  
5. Vanity AI metrics create roadmaps that never change a decision date.  
6. Tenancy and permission bugs outrank clever prompts for enterprise damage.  
7. Pull beats push when expert attention is the scarce resource.

---

## Refuse to ship

- Chatbot that cannot cite a system of record  
- Risk score without a prescribed next action and owner  
- Agent with write access before Crawl exit criteria are true  
- Productivity claim built only on self-report surveys  
- Maturity label ("AI-first") without a weekly override review  
- Second AI surface that forces copy-paste into the real workflow  

---

## Open problems (honest backlog)

- Evaluating prescribed actions (not only predicted labels) in production  
- Sharing health signals across product, CS, and account teams without a second truth  
- Keeping tenancy checks ahead of model novelty  
- Expert-facing explainability that survives audit  
- Funding last-mile integration with the same urgency as model access  
- Proving time-to-decision gains without inventing "hours saved" theater
